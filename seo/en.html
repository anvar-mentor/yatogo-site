Documents

Documents for company registration

The starting pack differs depending on whether the founder is an individual or a company.

Founder — an individual

  • Passports of the founders and the director
  • PINFL of the founders and the director
  • Digital signature for the founders and the director
  • Letter of guarantee for the legal address

Founder — a company

  • Legalised power of attorney from the parent company for the signatory of the charter documents
  • Legalised extract from the commercial register
  • Passports of the authorised representative and the director
  • PINFL and digital signature for the representative and the director

Common to both cases: a letter of guarantee providing the legal address with its cadastral number, and completing our intake form so we have the initial details of the company.

PINFL

The personal identification number of an individual.

Digital signature

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

How long does company registration take?

It depends on the package: Premium — from 2 business days, Business — 5, Classic — 10. The clock starts once the founders’ documents are collected and the PINFL and digital signature are issued.

Do I need to come to Uzbekistan in person?

The entity is registered online, but obtaining the PINFL and digital signature and opening the bank account require a visit — an account cannot be opened by power of attorney. We plan the trip so everything fits into 1–2 days.

How much charter capital is required?

For a JV LLC or foreign-owned LLC — from 400,000,000 UZS. A regular LLC with resident founders has no elevated requirement.

Which tax regime should I choose?

Simplified — 4% turnover tax, no office-size requirement. General — 12% VAT and 15% profit tax, office from 18 m². It depends on turnover and your clients: if they work with VAT, the general regime usually pays off. We advise during the consultation.

Where do I get a legal address?

You need an address with a cadastral number and a letter of guarantee from the owner. On the Business and Premium packages we prepare a comparison sheet of business centres so you can pick the right premises.

What documents are needed if the founder is a foreign company?

A legalised power of attorney from the parent company for the signatory, a legalised extract from the commercial register, passports of the representative and the director, and their PINFL and digital signatures.

How much do your services cost?

The price depends on the package and entity type. The state duty is paid separately and calculated in BRV. Leave a request and we will send an exact quote for your case.
